Color Harmony & Contrast

A visual exploration of dynamic themes driven by localized spatial color sampling. As you navigate the image, the background, text, and accents adapt in real-time.

Understanding Complements

Complementary colors are pairs of colors which, when combined or mixed, cancel each other out by producing a grayscale color like white or black. When placed next to each other, they create the strongest contrast for those two colors.

"Color does not add a pleasant quality to design - it reinforces it."
— Pierre Bonnard

In digital interfaces, relying purely on opposite hues can lead to visual vibration. The key is to manage the Lightness and Chroma (saturation) to maintain readability while keeping the aesthetic link.

Contrast & Context

The background color naturally shifts based on the overall brightness of the viewport. Darker image areas trigger a subtle dark mode, while lighter areas return to a luminous white, maintaining perfect text contrast automatically.

Dynamic Background

Calculated by averaging the lightness of the viewport and applying a faint hue of the dominant color to unify the design.

Responsive Accents

Accent colors are verified against the background. If contrast falls below WCAG standards, lightness is dynamically corrected.

The Mechanics

This demonstration does not simply average the entire image. It utilizes an OffscreenCanvas to render the exact viewport you are looking at.

  • 1 Extract dominant neutral tones to formulate subtle background tints and determine Light/Dark mode.
  • 2 Identify vibrant peaks for primary accents, ensuring they pop against the current background.
  • 3 Calculate a 180° hue shift for the complementary accent to provide secondary highlights.
  • 4 Apply an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) to smooth the transition beautifully over time.
